Problem

I need practical, inspectable evidence of Linux troubleshooting as I move into a junior support or operations role. A list of tools does not show how I investigate a problem.

What I have built

A public repository with a seven-week roadmap, an evidence standard and templates for incident reports and runbooks. The structure makes space for scripts, diagrams, container labs and monitoring investigations as they are completed.

How I troubleshoot

  1. Understand the symptom and collect evidence.
  2. Form a hypothesis and run a controlled test.
  3. Identify the cause; fix safely or escalate.
  4. Verify recovery and check for side effects.
  5. Document the result and what I learned.

Learning workflow

A simplified practice workflow. This diagram is not a claim about deployed lab hardware.

Public evidence

What I am learning

Connecting Linux service state, logs and network checks to a testable hypothesis, then explaining the result in a concise support note.

Current limitations
